# FN Transitions — Content Editing Guide

Every word on the FN Transitions site can be edited in the browser, with no technical
skills, at **`<site address>/studio`** (for example, fntransitions.vercel.app/studio).

## Logging in

1. Go to the site and enter the site password.
2. Add `/studio` to the address and press Enter.
3. Sign in with the email invitation you received (choose **E-mail / password** on the
   login screen; use "Forgot password?" the first time to set your password).

## What you can edit

| In the Studio | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Course** | The course title and the order of modules and foundation topics |
| **Modules** | Each module's title and the order of its lessons |
| **Lessons** | Every lesson: its title and all of its content — paragraphs, headings, lists, videos, reflection questions, and highlight boxes |
| **Home page** | All text on the front page: hero, resource cards, about section, pillars, FAQ answers, calls to action |
| **Program overview page** | All text on the program page, including the module cards and FAQs |
| **Modules & lessons page** | The headings and intro on the lesson list page |

## Making a change

1. Click the document you want (for example a lesson).
2. Edit the text directly. Formatting tools (bold, italic, links, headings) appear when
   you select text.
3. Press **Publish** (bottom right). Unpublished edits are drafts — visitors never see
   them until you publish.
4. Refresh the website. Your change appears within about a minute.

## Things to know

- **Fields marked "do not change"** (web addresses/slugs) keep links working — leave them.
- **Highlight boxes** have a colour picker and an optional heading.
- **Videos** take a YouTube *embed* link (youtube.com/embed/...).
- **Reflection questions** are a simple list — add, remove, or reorder freely.
- Deleting a lesson removes it from the course navigation. If that happens by accident,
  contact support — content can be restored from history.
- The site design (colours, fonts, layout, footer) is not editable here by design;
  design changes go through the developer.
